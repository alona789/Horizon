## [OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Astra, Scoring 99.9% on ARC-AGI-3](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/) ⭐️ 10.0/10

OpenAI announced GPT-6 Astra, its new flagship model, reporting a 99.9% score on the ARC-AGI-3 benchmark and major gains on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index. A system card was published at deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-astra. Because ARC-AGI-3 is designed to test interactive reasoning, exploration, and continuous learning, a near-perfect result pushes the conversation about OpenAI&\#x27;s models closer to questions of general intelligence. The release could reset expectations for frontier labs and intensify debates over whether current benchmarks truly measure AGI progress. The 99.9% ARC-AGI-3 figure was obtained using a Responses API harness; one commenter notes that GPT-5.6 Sol would score roughly 30% with the same setup, raising questions about fair comparison. Other standard benchmarks reportedly improved only modestly, making the release look more like a point update than a generational leap on those metrics.

**Background**: ARC-AGI-3 is an interactive reasoning benchmark that challenges AI agents to explore novel environments, acquire goals on the fly, build adaptable world models, and learn continuously; unlike the original ARC-AGI-1 from 2019, it moves beyond abstract visual puzzles toward agentic, open-ended tasks. OpenAI system cards are detailed safety reports published before deployment, covering external red teaming, frontier risk evaluations, and mitigations under the company&\#x27;s Preparedness Framework. The Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index is a separate composite evaluation that averages coding-agent pass@1 across benchmarks such as DeepSWE, Terminal-Bench, and SWE-Atlas-QnA.

The U.S. government filed an amicus brief in a Manhattan federal court supporting OpenAI, saying that training large language models on copyrighted content generally qualifies as fair use. It marks the government&\#x27;s first official position on AI training and copyright disputes. Because it is the first formal U.S. government statement on this issue, the brief could shape pending litigation, agency guidance, and congressional debates over AI regulation. The non-binding opinion may also embolden AI companies in similar copyright lawsuits. The brief was submitted in a case brought by The New York Times, which accused OpenAI and Microsoft in 2023 of using millions of its articles without permission to train ChatGPT. The Times criticized the government for siding with &\#x27;a few trillion-dollar AI companies&\#x27; at the expense of creators.

## [1993 Amiga Assembly Game Ported to Godot with LLM Assistance](https://babyloniantwins.com/blog/porting-a-1993-amiga-game-to-godot/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A developer has documented how he ported his 1993 Amiga game, originally written in MC68000 assembly in Baghdad, to Godot during last July&\#x27;s holiday using Claude. He first had the model reassemble the original code with vasm until the binaries were byte-identical, then completed the port in an evening. This demonstrates that LLMs are now capable of genuinely sophisticated reverse-engineering and porting work on decades-old assembly code, lowering the barrier to preserving or modernizing retro games. It also offers a practical workflow that combines LLM-generated code with traditional assembler verification, which could inspire similar projects for other forgotten titles. An important technical twist is that the original shipped binaries were memory snapshots saved after the game had already been run, so they were not clean AsmOne output; this caused a persistent 108-byte mismatch that the model had to investigate. The article&\#x27;s first draft was also written by Claude and then edited line by line by the developer, who has also released the original game for free.

hackernews · rabahs · Sep 3, 14:28 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49550375)

**Background**: The Amiga was a family of 16/32-bit personal computers introduced by Commodore in 1985 and widely used for games and creative software. Many Amiga games were written directly in MC68000 assembly, the instruction set of the Motorola 68000 processor at the heart of the machine. vasm is a portable, retargetable assembler commonly used today to assemble code for older platforms, while AsmOne was a popular Amiga assembler of that era. Godot is the modern open-source game engine that the author used as the port target.

Because Google accounts often hold years of email, calendars, and access to government and essential services, linking AI product bans to the entire account is seen as highly user-hostile. The clarification and promised ToS rewrite matter for trust in Google&\#x27;s AI products, especially as European eID systems increasingly rely on Google and Apple accounts. Antigravity is Google&\#x27;s agentic development platform combining a chat-oriented development environment, an IDE, a CLI, and an SDK for orchestrating autonomous AI agents, with code suggestions powered by Gemini. The controversy concerned a clause that, read literally, appeared to permit suspension of the entire Google account for third-party AI usage; the team says the actual scope is limited to the Antigravity account, and a ToS wording update is planned.
