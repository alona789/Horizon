## [First Stargardt&\#x27;s disease patient receives PRIMA retinal implant and reads a word](https://science.xyz/news/stargardt-first-rehab/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Science Corporation announced that the first Stargardt&\#x27;s disease patient to receive its PRIMA retinal implant has regained enough vision to recognize letters and, by the second rehabilitation session, read a word. The surgery took place in May at the Sydney Eye Hospital in Australia, and the result was disclosed by CEO Max Hodak. Stargardt disease blinds roughly 150,000 people worldwide and has no cure, so demonstrating that a photovoltaic retinal prosthesis can restore letter and word recognition in this inherited condition — not just in age-related macular degeneration — meaningfully widens the population that such implants could eventually help. It also strengthens the case for subretinal photovoltaic chips as a platform therapy across outer-retinal degenerations. PRIMA is a 2×2 mm subretinal chip with 378 near-infrared-sensitive photovoltaic pixels that is paired with camera-equipped glasses and a pocket computer to stimulate surviving inner retinal cells. This particular implant was performed under a clinical trial rather than the device&\#x27;s approved indication, and reading a single word is an early rehabilitation milestone for one patient, not evidence of restored fluent reading.

**Background**: Stargardt disease is the most common inherited single-gene retinal disease, usually caused by bi-allelic mutations in the ABCA4 gene, and it progressively destroys the macula starting in childhood or adolescence. Because it damages the outer retina \(the photoreceptors\) while the inner retinal layers often remain intact, subretinal photovoltaic implants like PRIMA can in principle bypass the lost cells and re-create visual signals. PRIMA was originally developed by Pixium Vision and is now being advanced by Science Corporation, the company founded in 2021 by former Neuralink co-founder Max Hodak; the news item notes the device is already approved in Europe for age-related macular degeneration.

## [xAI launches Grok Voice 3.0, topping speech-to-speech index](https://x.ai/news/grok-voice-3) ⭐️ 8.0/10

xAI released Grok Voice 3.0, a speech-to-speech model that it says ranks first on the Artificial Analysis Speech-to-Speech Index with a score of 86.9, combined with sub-1-second latency and a price of $0.08 per minute that matches the previous generation. The model is available through the API as of today and already powers millions of daily conversations at Tesla and SpaceX, while Starlink customer support handles roughly 20,000 calls per day with about 70% resolved without a human agent. This is a concrete, production-scale entry into the fast-growing voice-agent market from a major AI lab, and the combination of a top index ranking, sub-second latency and unchanged pricing puts direct pressure on rival speech-to-speech providers. Enterprises building customer-support and telephony agents now have a benchmarked alternative that has already been validated on high-volume, real-world traffic inside Musk&\#x27;s own companies. The score of 86.9 on the Artificial Analysis Speech-to-Speech Index is an equal-weighted composite of four components — speech reasoning \(Big Bench Audio\), agentic performance \(tau-Voice\), arena preference and task success rate — and a model must report results in all four to receive a score at all. Pricing stays at $0.08 per minute, so the improvement comes without a cost increase, and the headline automation figure of roughly 70% still implies that roughly three in ten Starlink calls need a human.

**Background**: Speech-to-speech models differ from older voice pipelines that chain separate speech-recognition, language-model and text-to-speech stages: they process audio in and audio out natively, which cuts latency and preserves tone and conversational dynamics such as turn-taking and interruption handling. The Artificial Analysis Speech-to-Speech Index, announced in mid-2026, was created to give a single comparable measure of how well these native models perform across reasoning, agentic tasks and user preference. Voice agents are a key battleground for AI labs because customer support and call centers are among the highest-volume, most immediately monetizable applications of conversational AI.
