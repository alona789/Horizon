## [CDD recovers finetuning data from logits without weight access](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1umn2dk/contrastive_decoding_diffing_cdd_recovering/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Contrastive Decoding Diffing (CDD) is a novel model diffing method that recovers verbatim finetuning data from language models using only logit access, without needing weights, activations, or a probe corpus. It achieves a verbatim recovery score of 4/5 or higher on 19 out of 20 model pairs across four model families (1B to 32B parameters) on the SDF benchmark. This method significantly lowers the barrier for extracting private finetuning data, raising critical privacy and security concerns for LLM deployment. It outperforms the whitebox Activation Difference Lens (ADL) method, which requires full weight access but never exceeds a score of 3/5 on the same benchmark. A single default configuration works across all tested models without per-model calibration or layer selection. An unplanned finding revealed that the name 'Dr. Elena Rodriguez' appeared across multiple finetuning domains because it was favored by Claude Sonnet 3.6 in synthetic data generation.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/CebulkaZapiekana · Jul 3, 19:01

**Background**: Model diffing aims to identify and explain differences between a base model and a finetuned version. Contrastive Decoding (CD) is a decoding method that contrasts logits from a large and small model to improve text quality. Activation Difference Lens (ADL) uses activation differences to steer generation but requires full weight access and only recovers vague domain-level descriptions, not verbatim data.

## [NASA launches private rescue satellite to save Swift telescope](https://apnews.com/article/swift-nasa-satellite-rescue-katalyst-a7ddd740ca099587c58865f583c7245a) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On July 3, 2026, NASA launched the LINK spacecraft aboard a Northrop Grumman Pegasus XL rocket to rendezvous with the aging Swift space telescope, which is in a decaying orbit due to increased solar activity and could re-enter Earth's atmosphere as early as October 2026. This mission marks the first time a private spacecraft will attempt to capture and boost a U.S. government satellite, demonstrating a new capability for on-orbit servicing that could help reduce space debris and extend the life of valuable assets. LINK will use a robotic arm to grab Swift and then fire thrusters to raise its orbit by approximately 240 kilometers. If successful, Swift could resume science observations as early as September 2026.

**Background**: The Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory, launched in 2004, studies gamma-ray bursts and other cosmic phenomena. Over two decades, its orbit has decayed due to atmospheric drag, especially during solar maximum. Without intervention, it would undergo uncontrolled re-entry by the end of 2026. This mission is part of a growing trend of commercial satellite servicing, exemplified by companies like Northrop Grumman.
