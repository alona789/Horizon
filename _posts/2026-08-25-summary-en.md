## [Apple unveils M6 and M5 Ultra, delivering major performance and AI gains](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-m6-and-m5-ultra-for-a-big-leap-in-performance-and-ai-compute/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On August 25, 2026, Apple introduced the M6, its first 2nm chip with a 12-core CPU, 12-core GPU, and dual 16-core Neural Engine, alongside the M5 Ultra, the company&\#x27;s most powerful chip ever, featuring a first-of-its-kind quad-die architecture. This announcement marks a significant leap in Mac performance and on-device AI compute, positioning Apple silicon to handle increasingly demanding pro and AI workloads. It also intensifies competition with other chipmakers and signals Apple&\#x27;s continued commitment to its custom silicon roadmap. The M5 Ultra is built by linking two dual-die M5 Max chips via next-generation UltraFusion technology, marking the first quad-die architecture in an M-series SoC. The M6, meanwhile, is Apple&\#x27;s first 2nm chip, with a larger 12-core CPU, 12-core GPU, and Dual 16-core Neural Engine for improved power efficiency.

OpenAI has released first benchmark data for its custom Broadcom-built Jalapeño inference ASIC, claiming 1.5–1.9x higher AI throughput per megawatt and 1.7–3.6x lower end-to-end latency than Nvidia&\#x27;s GB300 across three large models. The chip is slated for deployment in OpenAI&\#x27;s own data centers by the end of this year. This marks a potential inflection point in AI infrastructure: a hyperscaler&\#x27;s custom inference chip claims to beat Nvidia&\#x27;s flagship commercial GPU on the metrics that matter most for serving large language models. ## [FDA Authorizes First Wearable Device Continuously Monitoring Both Ketone and Blood Sugar Levels](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-authorizes-first-wearable-device-continuously-monitors-both-ketone-levels-and-blood-sugar) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The FDA has authorized the Libre Duo 10 Day Continuous Dual Glucose Ketone Monitoring System, the first wearable device that continuously measures both ketone and glucose levels in interstitial fluid for people aged 2 years and older with diabetes. Readings are sent wirelessly to a compatible smartphone every minute, day and night. Combining continuous ketone and glucose monitoring in one wearable can help detect diabetic ketoacidosis earlier, a potentially fatal complication, while giving patients a practical metabolic health tool. This regulatory milestone may also accelerate the broader adoption of multi-biomarker wearables and push improvements in reimbursement and access. The system measures interstitial fluid just beneath the skin every minute and is the first FDA-authorized wearable ketone monitor as well as the first combined glucose-ketone wearable. It builds on Abbott&\#x27;s FreeStyle Libre 3 CGM sensor design and previously received FDA breakthrough device designation.

hackernews · sunnynagra · Aug 25, 19:07 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49439017)

**Background**: Ketones are chemicals produced by the liver when the body burns fat for energy; they rise to dangerous levels in diabetic ketoacidosis, a potentially fatal condition. Traditional ketone testing uses urine or blood strips, which only provide episodic readings, while continuous glucose monitoring has become common. Continuous ketone sensing has only recently been shown to be feasible using interstitial fluid and microneedle sensors. This authorization reflects a broader trend toward wearable devices that track multiple metabolic biomarkers at once.

## [Firefox 157 to Enable JPEG XL by Default on All Platforms](https://groups.google.com/a/mozilla.org/g/dev-platform/c/3YMV4MS34KA?pli=1) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Mozilla has announced that Firefox 157 will enable JPEG XL by default on all platforms, marking a major milestone in web image format adoption. This follows active discussion about cross-browser support, with Chromium also reportedly moving in the same direction. Firefox&\#x27;s default support for JPEG XL could accelerate adoption of this modern image format across the web, benefiting users with better compression and higher quality images. It also puts pressure on other browsers like Chrome and Safari to align, potentially reshaping the image format landscape. JPEG XL is an open standard \(ISO/IEC 18181\) offering both lossy and lossless compression, wide color gamut, HDR, and high bit depth support. Notably, both Firefox and Chromium are using the Rust-based jxl-rs library, while Apple has already shipped libjxl written in C++.

hackernews · yboris · Aug 25, 17:55 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49437946)

**Background**: JPEG XL is a modern image format developed by the Joint Photographic Experts Group, Google, and Cloudinary, designed to meet the needs of web image delivery and professional photography. It outperforms older formats like JPEG and PNG in compression efficiency while supporting advanced features such as layers and up to 4099 channels. Browser support has been uneven: Chromium previously removed JPEG XL support in 2023, but recent signs suggest it may be reconsidering, partly due to Mozilla&\#x27;s push with Rust-based implementations.

## [EVE Online Begins Python 3 Migration With futurize Across 2.4 Million Lines](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/25/eve-online-move-to-python-3/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

EVE Online has announced the start of its long-awaited migration from Stackless Python 2.7 to Python 3. The first step is running the futurize script over 2.4 million lines of code, followed by manual review of roughly 20,000 spots where Python 2 and Python 3 behave differently. This is one of the largest and most publicly documented legacy Python migrations, showing how a major game studio approaches an aging, mission-critical codebase. It also highlights the continued importance of Python 3 adoption and the real-world effort required to keep massive systems alive long after Python 2&\#x27;s end of life. The post gives concrete examples of behavior differences, such as integer division: 1 / 2 returns 0 in Python 2 but 0.5 in Python 3. It does not announce how Stackless Python will be replaced, although EVE Frontier&\#x27;s Carbon engine already uses the open-source carbonengine/scheduler library.

rss · Simon Willison · Aug 25, 22:59

**Background**: Stackless Python is a fork of CPython that adds microthreads called tasklets, enabling highly concurrent code without the overhead of operating-system threads; EVE Online has relied on it since 2003. futurize, part of the Python-Future project, automatically converts Python 2 code toward Python 3 compatibility by applying fixers and adding future imports, but developers still have to manually resolve semantic differences. EVE Online&\#x27;s last major interpreter upgrade was to Stackless Python 2.7 in 2010, making this move a significant modernization milestone.
